## One paragraph
This study aims to define a rupture-risk index based on a 1,000-sample cost-effective FSI study focused on coronary plaque rupture risk. The goal is to identify the factors that drive this index, providing insights into the mechanisms behind plaque rupture.

## Novelty
- 발견 1: Introduction of a new rupture-risk index (ΔPSS / strength)
- 발견 2: Cost-effective approach using a large sample size (1,000 samples)
- 발견 3: Focus on the drivers of coronary plaque rupture risk
