## One paragraph
This review integrates the state of the art across three axes of precision weeding—chemical, physical, and image-AI—through the lens of detection–actuation matching. We formalize this concept with a latency-budget model and analyze 132 studies to compare efficacy, cost, and environmental impact. We highlight the bottleneck of detection versus actuation and propose design archetypes based on herbicide selectivity, using garlic and onion as a case study to identify gaps in academic validation.
