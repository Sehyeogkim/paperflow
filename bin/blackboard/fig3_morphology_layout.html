Figure 3. Idealized coronary plaque model and its morphological parameters. (a) Solid-domain composition of the two phenotypes: LAP comprises vessel wall, fibrous cap (FC) and lipid core; CP adds a calcification domain. (b) Definition of the morphological input variables on a transverse cross-section: lumen diameter Dlum, wall thickness twall, FC thickness tFC, lipid arc angle θlipid, plaque burden, and calcification arc θcalc (CP). (c) Longitudinal section showing lipid-pool axial length Llipid and the globally-constant FC thickness assumed along the cap. Variable ranges and distributions are listed in Table 1.

Why this layout (your question)

  • Main text, not appendix. No page limit in a thesis, and these definitions are load-bearing for §3.2 (VI) and the results.
  • One figure, multiple panels — not one crowded panel. a = domains, b = cross-section vars, c = axial.
  • Geometry on the figure, numbers in Table 1. That split is what keeps it from looking overloaded.
  • Modular → if you later submit to a journal with page limits, keep (b) in main text, push (c) to supplementary.
