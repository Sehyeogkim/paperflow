A rupture-risk index for coronary plaque: definition and sensitivity analysis

Jeff Kim · Advisor · KAIST, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering

Abstract

We define a rupture-risk index as the ratio of peak structural stress range (ΔPSS) to material strength, and quantify its sensitivity across 1,000 lipid-rich and calcified plaque samples using a cost-effective pulsatile FSI framework. A global Sobol analysis ranks material properties above hemodynamic and morphological factors.

2. Method

2.1 Cost-effective pulsatile FSI

A snapshot-based fluid–structure coupling, validated in prior work [12], keeps a 1,000-sample parametric study tractable while reproducing wall-stress fields.

2.2 Parametric dataset

Lipid-rich and calcified phenotypes were sampled across the ranges in geometry_params.csv; the index is stress / strength, with stress taken as ΔPSS (Fig. 6).
