2.1 Cost-effective pulsatile FSI grounded

A snapshot-based fluid–structure coupling validated in prior work @kim_snapshot is employed here to make 1,000-sample parametric analysis tractable.

LAP (3-domain) and CP (4-domain) phenotypes; inputs sampled over ranges in data/geometry_params.csv. Rupture-risk index = stress / strength, stress = ΔPSS Fig 6.
